## Executive Summary

You are a **solo technical founder**, pre-company, pre-revenue, building an **AI crypto-trading SaaS** with Claude Code. The right strategy is **NOT** "incorporate + GST + lawyers now." It is:

1. **Validate & get to first paying interest BEFORE spending on a company.** Incorporation, GST, compliance cost money + time and create obligations. Don't take them on until the product is proven and someone wants to pay.
2. **Stay non-custodial forever** (users trade their *own* accounts with *their own* keys — already how it's built). This single decision avoids the most dangerous regulation (custody, PMS/AIF, fund management).
3. **Position as software/automation, never as advice or guaranteed returns.** This is a legal survival issue, not marketing taste.
4. **Confirm a payment processor will even accept you EARLY** — most global processors (Stripe/Paddle/Lemon Squeezy) restrict crypto-trading software. This can block your whole business model; de-risk it first.
5. **Incorporate a Private Limited only when revenue/funding justifies it** (likely Phase 4–5).

**Biggest risks (ranked):** ① payment processor rejection · ② regulatory reclassification as "investment advice/PMS" · ③ a marketing/claims violation · ④ over-spending on formation too early.

## Phase detail

### Phase 0 — Idea validation (₹0)
- **Deliverables:** problem/solution one-pager; 20+ conversations with target crypto users; a landing page with a waitlist; **a written email to Razorpay/Cashfree + Paddle asking if they accept "crypto trading automation SaaS"** (de-risk the #1 blocker now).
- **Risks:** building something nobody pays for; discovering processors won't accept you *after* building billing.
- **Exit:** ≥20 "yes I'd pay", and at least one viable payment path identified.

### Phase 1 — Development (₹0–5k)
- **Deliverables:** V2 baseline + **P0 Feature Store + P1 Risk Manager** (capital preservation first), paper mode polished. Cheap/free dev model.
- **Risks:** over-building features before validation; scope creep.
- **Exit:** the bot runs safely in paper with the risk engine enforcing limits.

### Phase 2 — Private testing — just you (₹0–6k)
- **Deliverables:** run it on *your own* small live account after long paper; tune; write the setup guide + risk disclosure draft.
- **Risks:** real-money bugs → only test with money you can lose; keep it tiny.
- **Exit:** 30+ days stable; you trust it with your own funds.

### Phase 3 — Closed beta, 5–20 users (₹0–10k)
- **Deliverables:** invite-only; everyone **paper-first**; collect feedback + testimonials; draft ToS/Privacy/Risk docs; basic support (email/Telegram).
- **Risks:** a beta user loses money and blames you → **mandatory risk disclosure + non-custodial + "use at own risk" consent**; no money handling yet (free beta).
- **Exit:** 10 engaged users, clear value proven, zero legal/fund incidents.

### Phase 4 — Company registration (₹15–35k + ~₹30k/yr)
- **Deliverables:** **Private Limited Company** (see [Company Formation](./COMPANY_FORMATION_GUIDE.md)); business bank account; GST + **LUT** (export of services); payment gateway approved + integrated; legal docs published; DPIIT/Startup India recognition (free, unlocks tax holiday).
- **Risks:** processor underwriting rejection; choosing wrong structure; compliance you forget (hire a CA).
- **Exit:** you can legally **take money** from India + abroad.

### Phase 5 — First revenue (covered by Phase 4 spend)
- **Deliverables:** turn on paid plans; convert beta → paid; refund policy live; invoicing/GST automated.
- **Risks:** churn, payment failures, chargebacks → dunning + clear refunds.
- **Exit:** 5–10 paying customers, MRR > all monthly running costs.

### Phase 6 — Public launch (₹20–80k marketing)
- **Deliverables:** marketing site (pricing/features/FAQ/docs), content/SEO, ProductHunt/community, referral program.
- **Risks:** scaling support; a public claim violation → strict marketing review.
- **Exit:** 50–100 customers, churn < 8%/mo.

### Phase 7 — Global expansion (varies)
- **Deliverables:** multi-region readiness ([Global doc](./GLOBAL_EXPANSION_MASTER_PLAN.md)); MoR or regional tax handling; multi-currency pricing; localized risk/legal.
- **Risks:** per-region regulation (UK/US retail crypto promos are strict) → start with permissive markets.
- **Exit:** paying users in 2–3 regions, tax handled.

### Phase 8 — Scale 1000+ (reinvest)
- **Deliverables:** first hires (support, then eng); ops playbook; SOC2-lite/security maturity; maybe raise.
- **Risks:** founder burnout, ops chaos → systemize before scaling.
- **Exit:** 1000 customers, durable margins, a small team.
